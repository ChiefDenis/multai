## Simple Icons license

Simple Icons is released under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
The SVG paths are reproduced unchanged from the upstream icons.

## Monochrome marks

`chatgpt.svg`, `claude.svg`, and `kimi.svg` are monochrome brand marks. They are
tinted to the current foreground colour at render time so that the same
authentic artwork remains legible on both light and dark backgrounds. No other
logo is recoloured. Coloured marks keep their original brand colours and
proportions.
